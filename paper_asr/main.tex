\section{Per-month statistics and calibration}\label{app:months}
Table~\ref{tab:months} lists the per-month exposure counts, mapping-set size, fitted energy-scale parameters and the source of the particle template. \Cref{fig:fwhmtemp} shows the dependence of the line width on detector temperature that motivates the temperature-binned calibration.

\begin{figure}[!h]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{figures/fig_fwhm_temp.pdf}
\caption{Al K$\alpha$ FWHM against detector temperature (TEMP) from the temperature-binned calibration of each month (one point per temperature bin, at its median TEMP), coloured by year. Within a month the line width increases steeply with temperature (4--6\,eV per \textdegree C); at a fixed temperature of $-40$\,\textdegree C it increases from about 137\,eV in 2021 to 171\,eV in 2026, and the temperature slope steepens, as expected from radiation damage.}\label{fig:fwhmtemp}
\end{figure}
