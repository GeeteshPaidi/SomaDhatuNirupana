We present a processing chain for Chandrayaan-2 CLASS X-ray fluorescence spectra that is reproducible entirely from public data. Each \SI{8}{s} spectrum is fitted on raw counts with a Poisson likelihood. The model includes Sherman primary fluorescence with matrix attenuation and incidence geometry, a scattered solar spectrum, a binned Gaussian detector response, the published filter transmission, and a background with two components. The incident solar spectrum is an isothermal CHIANTI spectrum at the GOES XRS temperature of each exposure. Solar lines carry \LineFracSiLo--\LineFracMgHi\% of the flux that excites Mg, Al and Si, and Mg/Si and Al/Si change by tens of per cent for realistic temperature errors. We also identify an instrumental Al K$\alpha$ component, excited by sunlight reaching the instrument, that appears only when the spacecraft is sunlit; it must be separated from the particle background. Synthetic tests recover Mg/Si and Al/Si without bias, while Ca/Si, Ti/Si and Fe/Si are not recoverable without a measured solar spectrum. From \NRawFiles{} exposures in \NMonths{} monthly archives, \NMapped{} spectra pass pre-declared quality criteria and give global 2\textdegree{} Mg/Si and Al/Si maps with observation-count and uncertainty layers and no infill. The maps correlate with Lunar Prospector GRS ratios ($r=\GrsMgR$ for Mg/Si and $\GrsAlR$ for Al/Si on 5\textdegree{} pixels). Comparison with nine landing sites is inconclusive at the $25\times37$\,km footprint scale.
