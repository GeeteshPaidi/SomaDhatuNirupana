We test whether solar spectra inferred from GOES can recover stable lunar Mg/Si and Al/Si patterns from public observations of the Chandrayaan-2 Large Area Soft X-ray Spectrometer (CLASS). Each \SI{8}{s} spectrum is fitted on raw counts with a Poisson likelihood and a simplified fundamental-parameter model including matrix effects, incidence geometry, scattered solar X-rays, the distributed ancillary response and a particle background. The solar spectrum of each exposure is an isothermal CHIANTI spectrum at the temperature from the GOES X-Ray Sensor (XRS). Because solar lines carry \LineFracSiLo--\LineFracMgHi\% of the flux that excites Mg, Al and Si, element/Si ratios depend appreciably on this spectrum. In synthetic tests at the correct solar temperature (\SI{5}{MK}) and at the tested signal levels (300--10{,}000 Si K$\alpha$ counts), Mg/Si and Al/Si are recovered with median biases of at most \SynMaxBiasMgAl\%; Ca/Si, Ti/Si and Fe/Si are not constrained. From \NRawFiles{} exposures in \NMonths{} monthly archives, \NMapped{} spectra pass the quality criteria and give global 2\textdegree{} maps of model-derived elemental weight ratios without infill. The mapped ratios correlate with Lunar Prospector GRS estimates on 5\textdegree{} pixels ($r=\GrsAlR$ for Al/Si, \GrsMgR{} for Mg/Si) and with nine landing-site soils ($r=\SiteAlTwoR$ and \SiteMgTwoR), but are systematically higher than the soil values, so the maps are relative. Maps built separately from February 2022 -- December 2023 and November 2024 -- May 2026 correlate at $r=\EpMgTwoR$ (Mg/Si) and \EpAlTwoR{} (Al/Si) on common 2\textdegree{} cells, with median levels within 1\%. For three months, \XsmNClass{} spectra overlap Chandrayaan-2 Solar X-ray Monitor (XSM) observations; among them, successful refits with XSM-derived solar spectra shift the median Al/Si by \XsmTwoAl{} to \XsmOneAl\% and Mg/Si by \XsmTwoMg{} to \XsmOneMg\%.
