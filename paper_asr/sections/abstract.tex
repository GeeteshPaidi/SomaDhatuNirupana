We present a processing chain for Chandrayaan-2 CLASS X-ray fluorescence spectra that is reproducible from public data. Each \SI{8}{s} spectrum is fitted on raw counts with a Poisson likelihood, using Sherman primary fluorescence with matrix attenuation and incidence geometry, a scattered solar spectrum, a binned Gaussian detector response, the published filter transmission, and a particle-background template. The incident solar spectrum is an isothermal CHIANTI spectrum at the GOES XRS temperature of each exposure. Solar lines carry \LineFracSiLo--\LineFracMgHi\% of the flux that excites Mg, Al and Si, so element/Si ratios change by tens of per cent for realistic temperature errors. We also identify an instrumental Al K$\alpha$ component that appears only when the Sun illuminates the spacecraft from behind; if it is mixed into the background model, it corrupts Al/Si. Synthetic tests recover Mg/Si and Al/Si without bias, while Ca/Si, Ti/Si and Fe/Si are not recoverable without a measured solar spectrum. From \NRawFiles{} exposures in \NMonths{} monthly archives, \NMapped{} spectra pass pre-declared quality criteria and give global 2\textdegree{} maps with quality layers and no infill. Al/Si resolves individual maria and correlates with Lunar Prospector GRS on 5\textdegree{} pixels ($r=\GrsAlR$, about 80\% of the noise-limited ceiling) and with nine landing-site soils ($r=\SiteAlTwoR$). Mg/Si correlates with GRS ($r=\GrsMgR$); its landing-site test is inconclusive, as it is for GRS itself.
