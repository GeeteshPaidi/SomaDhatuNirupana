CLASS Level-1 data \citep{CLASSdata} and XSM data \citep{XSMdata} are available from the ISRO PRADAN portal. GOES XRS 1-minute science-quality data are available from NOAA/NCEI \citep{GOESdata}. The Lunar Prospector GRS elemental abundances are available from the PDS Geosciences Node \citep{PrettymanPDS2012}. The processing code; the per-footprint results of the quality-0 mapping set and of the second epoch; the maps of all map sets; the per-month detector calibrations and background templates; the validation, epoch and XSM comparison outputs; and the scripts that produce the numbers, tables and figures of this paper are available at \url{https://github.com/GeeteshPaidi/SomaDhatuNirupana} and archived on Zenodo \citep{SomaDhatu}. Table~5 and Figures~1, 4 and 7 can be regenerated from the archived products alone. The per-spectrum fit tables of all dayside spectra (about \SI{1.1}{GB}, including flagged fits) are available from the corresponding author on request.
