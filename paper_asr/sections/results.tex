\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Pipeline statistics}\label{sec:selection}
\Cref{tab:stages} traces the exposures through the pipeline. All \NDayside{} dayside exposures with valid geometry were fitted; no flux-percentile pre-selection was applied. \NMapped{} spectra (\PctMapped\% of the dayside set) pass every quality criterion and form the mapping set. The fraction passing varies strongly with solar activity. It ranges from 2\% in February 2021, near solar minimum, where most spectra fall below the count threshold or below GOES B1, to 66\% in February 2025 (\cref{tab:months}). The GOES temperatures of the mapped exposures have a median of \MapTmed{}\,MK (5th--95th percentile \MapTlo--\MapThi{}\,MK).

\subsection{Fit examples}\label{sec:examples}
\Cref{fig:fits} shows fits to a bright, a median and a faint dayside spectrum. The model reproduces the Mg, Al and Si K lines, the Ca K line and the scattered solar continuum and lines. In the faint spectrum the particle background dominates above \SI{2}{keV}, and the uncertainties on Mg/Si and Al/Si become correspondingly large. The fitted instrumental amplitude $\gamma$ is zero for most dayside spectra, consistent with the geometry dependence in \cref{fig:background}. Bright flare spectra (e.g.\ the top panel, $T=16.8$\,MK) often fail the goodness-of-fit criterion. Residual structure below \SI{2}{keV} points to limitations of the isothermal solar spectrum and of the line-shape model, so the mapping set is weighted towards non-flaring and moderate-flare conditions.

\subsection{Global maps}\label{sec:maps}
\Cref{fig:maps} shows the Mg/Si and Al/Si maps with their observation-count and standard-error layers. Over the mapping set, the median footprint ratios are Mg/Si $=\MedMg$ (5th--95th percentile \PfiveMg--\PninetyfiveMg) and Al/Si $=\MedAl$ (\PfiveAl--\PninetyfiveAl). The Mg/Si range lies within the range of returned lunar soils. The Al/Si median sits at the upper end of the soil range, as expected from the unmodelled SCD efficiency (\cref{sec:limitations}).

Quality-0 footprints reach every 2\textdegree{} cell (\CovUsedCells\% of cells, \CovUsedArea\% of the area), and \CovFiveArea\% of the surface is covered by cells with at least five footprints. Coverage is complete because the 24 archives include polar orbits at all local times. It is not produced by interpolation. The polar cells have the fewest footprints and the largest standard errors.

The Mg/Si map shows the expected first-order dichotomy: higher values over the nearside maria and the South Pole--Aitken basin, lower values over the farside feldspathic highlands. The Al/Si map shows the complementary pattern. Residual north--south striping follows individual orbits. It reflects orbit-to-orbit differences in the solar state that the GOES isothermal temperature does not fully capture (\cref{sec:discussion}).
